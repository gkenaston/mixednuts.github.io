Everything Your Group Needs

From scheduling to roster management, Mixed Nuts handles the logistics so you can focus on playing.

Recurring Schedules

Set up weekly, biweekly, or custom recurrence patterns. Matches are generated automatically so you never have to create them manually.

Smart Rosters

Players opt in or out of each match. Once the roster is full, additional players join an automatic reserve queue -- first come, first served.

Reserve Promotion

When a confirmed player drops out, the next reserve is automatically promoted and notified. No more scrambling to fill spots.

Substitute Pool

Invite subs per match without adding them as full members. Subs can claim open spots, get bumped when a regular returns, or opt out -- with admin notifications throughout.

Match Teams & Scores

On game day, sort confirmed players into teams on each court, field, or pitch, track scores by set, game, or inning, and post the lineup and final results straight to match chat.

Chat & Notification Inbox

Real-time group chat plus per-match chat for confirmed players, with thread replies and reactions. Tap the bell on the Home tab to revisit every roster change, game update, and reply — even ones you dismissed.
