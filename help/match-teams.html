Match Teams & Scores

Arrange your confirmed players into teams, track the score, and share it with the group.

What Are Match Teams?

Once your roster is set, the Teams tab on a match lets an organizer split the confirmed players into two sides on each playing surface -- a court, field, or pitch, depending on your sport. Each surface holds two sides, labeled Team A and Team B. How many players go on a side comes from your group's sport (for example, one per side for singles, five for basketball, nine for baseball), so a surface's capacity is players-per-side times two.

Building a Lineup

Open a match and tap the Teams tab. Each surface shows two sides with open slots:

  • Tap an open slot and pick a confirmed player to place them on that side.
  • A player can only be placed once -- anyone already assigned isn't offered for the remaining slots.
  • Tap a filled slot to swap the player out or clear it.
  • Tap the pencil beside a surface's name to rename it (for example, "Court 1" to "Center Court").

Only players confirmed on the roster can be added. If someone drops out after you've placed them, their slot is emptied and flagged so you can fill it again.

Rounds

For sports played across multiple rounds -- sets, games, or innings -- add a round to record the teams and score for each one. Use the round controls in the Teams tab to add or remove rounds; the label (Set, Game, Inning, and so on) follows your sport.

Tracking Scores

Scoring is optional and off by default. Turn it on from the menu on the Teams tab. With scoring enabled:

  • Each side gets a score stepper -- tap to raise or lower its score.
  • The winning side is highlighted so the result is easy to read at a glance.
  • You can turn scoring back off from the same menu if you don't need it.

Posting to Match Chat

Share the teams with everyone who's playing, straight from the Teams tab menu:

  • Post lineup to chat -- drops a lineup card into the match's chat showing the sides on each surface, so players know where they stand before they arrive.
  • Post scores to chat -- once scores are in, drops a score card with the final results and the winning side highlighted.

Both cards appear in match chat, visible to everyone confirmed on that match.

Who Can Edit

Building and editing teams, scores, and rounds is limited to the people who run the group -- the owner, admins, and the group leader. Everyone else on the roster sees the same lineup and scores as a read-only view, without the slot and score controls.
